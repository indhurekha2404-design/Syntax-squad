🧮 Important Formulae

Mean

\[ \text{Mean}=\frac{\text{Sum of Observations}}{\text{Number of Observations}} \]

Median

Middle value after arranging the data.

Mode

Most frequently occurring value.


📝 Worked Example

Data: 5, 8, 10, 12, 15

Mean \[ =\frac{5+8+10+12+15}{5}=10 \]

Median = 10

Mode = No Mode
