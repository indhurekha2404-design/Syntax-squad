🧮 Important Formulae

Area of Rectangle

\[ A=l\times b \]

Area of Triangle

\[ A=\frac12 bh \]

Circumference of Circle

\[ C=2\pi r \]

Area of Circle

\[ A=\pi r^2 \]


📝 Worked Example

Question:

Find the area of a triangle whose base is 10 cm and height is 8 cm.

\[ A=\frac12 bh \]

\[ =\frac12 \times10\times8 \]

\[ =40\text{ cm}^2 \]
