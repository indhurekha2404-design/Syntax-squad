🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Identify all four quadrants.
  • Plot ordered pairs.
  • Find distance between two points.
  • Calculate midpoint.
  • Understand straight-line equations.

📐 Important Formulae

Distance Formula

\[ d=\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2} \]

Midpoint Formula

\[ M\left(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right) \]

Slope

\[ m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1} \]

📝 Worked Example

Find the distance between A(2,3) and B(6,6).

\[ d=\sqrt{(6-2)^2+(6-3)^2} \]

\[ =\sqrt{16+9} =\sqrt{25}=5 \]
